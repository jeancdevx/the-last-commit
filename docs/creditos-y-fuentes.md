## Prompt de portada

Se usó generación integrada de imágenes con una composición original. El prompt fue:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a 3:2 wide key-art background for the original indie browser horror game The Last Commit. No text, no typography, no graphic layout, no characters, no logos. A late-2010s modest office on Christmas Eve at night, looking down a mundane narrow carpeted charcoal office corridor from eye level, one interstitial concrete frame. The only asymmetrical focal point at the right-center beyond the corridor is a scraggly artificial fir in an otherwise empty reception area. Tree has sparse warm tungsten fairy lights and clearly handmade off-white and kraft paper tags hanging individually on thin red thread; one larger ivory paper tag reflects a strong warm pool. Cool fluorescent ceiling rectangles, desaturated deep teal and gray in foreground, second desk slightly visible through interior frosted glass. Grounded cinematic 3D game concept render, matte material details and grounded lighting, subdued deep greens, sparse pale amber highlights, still and unnerving atmosphere. Leave enough uncluttered dark negative space across left upper and lower middle for separately placed browser title. Keep the tree readable and the ceiling lines visible, avoid crushing all shadows. No exterior snow, no classic haunted-house motifs, no monsters, no blood. Original composition.
